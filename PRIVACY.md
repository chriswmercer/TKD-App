# Privacy Policy – TKD Assistant

_Last updated: 2026-11-13_

TKD Assistant is a study aid for Tae Kwon-Do students. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- **No account, no sign-in.** The app has no user accounts.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.
- **No network use.** All syllabus content is bundled inside the app. The app works offline.
- **On-device storage only.** Your quiz scores are stored locally on your device so you can see your recent results. They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
